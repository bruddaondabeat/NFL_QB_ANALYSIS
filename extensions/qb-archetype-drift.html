QB Archetype Drift, 2021–2024

The core project found three QB archetypes in 2019–2023 data. This extension refits that clustering model across 2021–2024 — adding the unused 2024 season — and follows every quarterback’s movement between archetypes.

How QBs flowed between archetypes, season to season

Each band is a group of quarterbacks moving (or staying put) between archetypes in consecutive qualifying seasons (≥150 attempts). Hover a band to see exactly which QBs made that move.
