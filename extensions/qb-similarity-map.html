QB Similarity Engine

A nearest-neighbor “comps” model over 2019–2023 QB seasons: every season becomes a nine-feature style fingerprint, and the model answers a scouting-room question — who does this quarterback actually play like?

The QB style map

Every qualifying season (≥150 attempts) from the project’s 2019–2023 tracked cohort, projected onto two principal components of a nine-feature style fingerprint. Seasons that sit close together were statistically similar — hover any point for its three closest comps. Labels mark each QB’s most recent qualifying season; color is the archetype from the core project’s clustering model.
