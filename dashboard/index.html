Box scores tell you who threw for 4,000 yards. They don't tell you who holds up when the down changes, the clock runs out, and the season is on the line. This dashboard tracks situational pressure, clutch-window accuracy, 4th-down conversion trust, and machine-learned QB archetypes — rebuilt from nflverse play-by-play data.

Fine print

Methodology & Roadmap

Archetypes

KMeans (k=3) on completion %, yards/attempt, TD rate and INT rate, normalized across the window; minimum 200 attempts to qualify. Clusters are labeled by mean passer rating: Elite Quarterbacks, The League Core, Struggling & Backups.

Clutch window

Pass plays in the final two minutes of a half with the score inside one possession (−8 to +7). Rankings require 20+ clutch attempts and sort by a sample-adjusted rate (empirical Bayes: rates shrink toward the league average by sample size, so a hot dozen attempts can't headline). 4th-down conversion counts first downs gained, min 5 attempts, adjusted the same way.

Pressure test

Per-down completion percentage with a minimum of 20 attempts on each down. The delta is 3rd-down accuracy minus the QB's own 1st-down baseline — a within-player measure, so scheme and receiver quality mostly cancel out.

Data & cadence

All stats rebuilt from raw nflverse play-by-play via nflreadpy, using an attempt rule verified to reproduce official season stat lines exactly (100% match on Cmp/Att/Yds/TD/INT vs Pro Football Reference, 2021–24). A GitHub Actions pipeline re-pulls weekly in season (Tuesday mornings), recomputes every benchmark, and redeploys.

Deeper dives

The full analysis — EDA, time-series work, model selection, and a play-success prediction model — lives in the research notebook. Read it on nbviewer or see the Tableau executive summary.

Official records & contracts

ESPN Total QBR, 4th-quarter comebacks and game-winning drives on the Clutch Board are official records via Pro Football Reference (seasons 2021–24) — QBR is ESPN's proprietary model and is reported, not recomputed. Value Map contracts are a 2025-offseason snapshot (average annual value); performance is the full window.

The play model

The Play Lab runs a logistic regression on situational features (down, distance, field position, score, clock) over every regular-season pass in the window; success = positive expected points. Coefficients ship inside the page — no black box, no server. Accuracy is modestly above baseline by design: situation shifts the odds, it doesn't decide the play.

Reading the badges

Every section carries a stability badge from our year-over-year audit (2021–25, 117 QB season-pairs, official attempt rule): EPA/dropback is the stickiest metric at r ≈ 0.41, passer rating repeats at r ≈ 0.38, while individual 3rd-down deltas and 4th-down conversion rates don't persist at all (r ≈ 0) — so those boards are records of what happened, not projections.
